# BUG-003: Sound disappears after game interruption

## Summary
After the game is interrupted by a phone call, notification, or switching to another app,
audio does not resume when the player returns to Homescapes.
The game continues silently until audio settings are toggled or app is restarted.

## Environment
- Platform: iOS and Android
- Game version: latest production
- Reproducibility: could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4

## Severity
Medium

## Priority
Low

## Steps to Reproduce

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open Homescapes, confirm music and SFX are active |
| 2 | Press Home to send app to background |
| 3 | Wait 10 seconds |
| 4 | Return to Homescapes |

## Expected Result
Background music and sound effects resume automatically.

## Actual Result (original report)
Audio does not resume. Both music and SFX are silent.
Audio settings UI still shows them as enabled.

## Workaround
Toggle music or sound effects off and back on in the in-game settings menu.

## Reproduction Status
Could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4, Homescapes v6.x.
Audio resumed correctly after returning from background in all test runs.
Bug may have been fixed in a recent game update.

## Evidence Notes
iPhone does not record system audio from apps in screen recordings by default.
Video evidence could not be captured due to this system limitation.
