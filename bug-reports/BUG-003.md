# BUG-003: Sound disappears after game interruption

## Summary
After the game is interrupted by a phone call, notification, or switching to another app,
audio (music and sound effects) does not resume when the player returns.
The game continues silently until the audio settings are toggled or app is restarted.

## Environment
- Platform: iOS and Android
- Game version: latest production
- Reproducibility: frequent, not 100% consistent

## Severity
Medium

## Priority
Low

## Steps to Reproduce

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open Homescapes, confirm music and SFX are active |
| 2 | Trigger an interruption: incoming call, open another app, or lock screen |
| 3 | Return to Homescapes |

## Expected Result
Background music and sound effects resume automatically.
Audio state matches player settings.

## Actual Result
Audio does not resume. Both music and SFX are silent.
The audio settings UI still shows them as enabled.

## Workaround
Toggle music or sound effects off and back on in the in-game settings menu.

## Impact
Affects game experience. Players may assume audio is permanently broken.

## Source
Confirmed in official Playrix Help Center as a known issue.
