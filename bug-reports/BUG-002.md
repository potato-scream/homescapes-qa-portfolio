# BUG-002: Lives timer breaks when device date or time is changed manually

## Summary
When the player manually changes the date or time on their device,
the lives regeneration timer behaves incorrectly - it may freeze, show a wrong value,
or stop restoring lives entirely.

## Environment
- Platform: iOS and Android
- Game version: latest production
- Reproducibility: could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4

## Severity
Medium

## Priority
Medium

## Steps to Reproduce

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open Homescapes, ensure lives are below maximum |
| 2 | Note the lives timer countdown |
| 3 | Minimize the app |
| 4 | Open device Settings and manually change system time forward by 1 hour |
| 5 | Return to Homescapes |

## Expected Result
The lives timer continues correctly based on real elapsed time.

## Actual Result (original report)
The lives timer behaves incorrectly. Observed symptoms:
- Timer freezes and does not count down
- Timer displays an incorrect or negative value
- Lives stop regenerating until app is restarted

## Reproduction Status
Could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4, Homescapes v6.x.
Timer value before time change: 29:42.
Timer value after advancing device time by 1 hour: 27:22.
Timer continued counting down correctly. No incorrect behavior observed.
Bug may have been fixed in a recent game update.

## Impact
If present, players who change device time may permanently break lives regeneration
until they reinstall or restart the app.
