
## Summary
When a player receives an incoming phone call during an active match-3 level,
the level resets to the initial state upon returning to the game.
All moves made before the call are lost.

## Environment
- Platform: iOS and Android
- Game version: latest production
- Reproducibility: could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4

## Severity
High

## Priority
High

## Steps to Reproduce

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open Homescapes and start any match-3 level |
| 2 | Make several moves to progress into the level |
| 3 | Receive an incoming phone call (mobile network or VoIP) |
| 4 | Answer or decline the call |
| 5 | Return to the Homescapes app |

## Expected Result
The level resumes from the exact state it was in before the call.
Board layout and remaining move count are preserved. No lives are deducted.

## Actual Result (original report)
The level resets to its initial state. All moves made before the interruption are lost.
The player must start the level from the beginning.

## Reproduction Status
Could not reproduce on iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4, Homescapes v6.x.
Tested with two interruption types:
- Incoming mobile network call (tested 3 times)
- Incoming Telegram call (tested 3 times)
  Level progress was fully preserved in all 6 runs. No lives were deducted.
  Bug may have been fixed in a recent game update.

## Evidence Notes
iOS screen recording stops automatically when an incoming call is received.
Video evidence could not be captured due to this system limitation.
Screenshots taken before and after interruption are available in evidence/bug-001/.

## Impact
If present, players lose in-level progress due to normal device usage.
Especially frustrating on difficult levels.
